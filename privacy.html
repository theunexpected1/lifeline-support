Privacy policy

Updated 21 October 2026 · Publisher: Rahul Vagadiya
Contact: rahul.vagadiya@gmail.com

Your memories

Lifeline encrypts memory titles, stories, dates, places, private tags and notes, and attached media on your device before syncing. Cloudflare hosts encrypted records and files. Guest mode keeps memories on your device without an account. Guest data and unsynced changes can be lost if the app is deleted or the device is lost.

Your recovery code protects your account's encryption identity on a new device. Password reset alone cannot decrypt memories. Support cannot replace a lost recovery code. Anyone with both your account access and recovery code can access your encrypted memories.

Account and service information

Firebase Authentication processes your email address, Apple sign-in identity or password authentication, and verification or password-reset requests. Apple sign-in supports Hide My Email. Linking an Apple identity to an existing email account asks for your explicit consent. Account deletion reauthenticates with Apple and revokes Apple access when that provider is connected. Lifeline's service processes account identifiers, display names, a hash of your email for exact account lookup, public encryption and signing keys, encrypted recovery data, invitation and membership status, revision identifiers, timestamps, file sizes and integrity hashes. These routing and access-control details are not end-to-end encrypted.

When you enable push notifications, Apple supplies a device push token, which the service stores with your category preferences to deliver notifications. The app does not use advertising or third-party behavioral analytics. Hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, technical logs and security information under their own policies. Our application does not intentionally log memory content, passwords, recovery codes or identity tokens.

Sharing

Sharing requires an invitation to a verified account and explicit acceptance. An optional encrypted invitation preview can include the title and date before acceptance. Accepted participants receive access to the memory and its shared revision history. Private notes, tags, favourites and personal view preferences are encrypted separately for your account. Your identity-visibility choice controls how your name appears to other participants; the inviter already knows the account they invited. Compare safety fingerprints with recipients for sensitive sharing.

Removing, blocking or leaving a participant revokes server access and changes keys used for future contributions. Existing downloaded content cannot be remotely erased. Exports you choose to share are unencrypted and are outside Lifeline's control.

Device permissions and media

You select individual photos and videos using the system picker. Lifeline does not upload contacts or scan your entire photo library. Photos are resized and location metadata is removed. Video is prepared at up to 720p; only the first five minutes are retained after your confirmation. Temporary playback and export files are protected locally and cleaned up after use. Notification text is generic. App lock uses device authentication; Lifeline receives no biometric data.

Retention and deletion

Deleted memories have a 30-day restore window, after which the service removes their active records and media. Provider backups may expire later under the provider's retention policy. Signing out retains an encrypted, account-isolated device copy to protect unsynced work. Account deletion removes that account's local copy and keys, deletes Firebase sign-in access, removes recovery and device-token data from the service, revokes memberships and schedules owned memories for deletion. Minimal public signing information may remain to verify other participants' retained revision histories.

Invitations expire after seven days. Unfinished media reservations expire after 24 hours. Operational notification jobs expire after seven days. A storage-capacity pause stops new media uploads without automatically deleting existing memories.

Your choices and requests

In Settings you can manage notifications, Wi-Fi transfers, offline media, app lock, recovery and account deletion. Each memory offers export, private preferences and sharing controls. Guest-to-account migration asks before uploading encrypted guest memories. You can contact the publisher to request help with access, correction or deletion of account/service information. Please do not send private memories, passwords or recovery codes.

Service providers

Firebase Authentication: Firebase privacy and security. Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2: Cloudflare privacy policy. Apple provides platform services including TestFlight, device authentication and push delivery. Providers may process service information in countries outside your own.

Changes

This policy may change as Lifeline develops. This page will show the updated date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its beta release notes.
