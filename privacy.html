Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026 · Publisher: Rahul Vagadiya
Contact: rahul.vagadiya@gmail.com

Your memories

Lifeline encrypts memory titles, words, dates, places, private tags and notes, and attached photos and videos on your device before syncing. Only you and the people you share a memory with can open it. Cloudflare hosts the encrypted records and files. Without an account, memories stay on your device only and can be lost if the app is deleted or the device is lost.

Your account's encryption keys are stored in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end, so your own devices signed in to the same iCloud unlock your memories automatically. A recovery key in Settings unlocks them on any other device. A password reset alone cannot decrypt memories, and support cannot replace a lost recovery key.

Account and service information

Firebase Authentication processes your email address, Apple sign-in identity or password authentication, and verification or password-reset requests. Apple sign-in supports Hide My Email. Lifeline's service processes account identifiers, display names, a hash of your email for exact account lookup, public encryption and signing keys, encrypted recovery data, membership status, revision identifiers, timestamps, file sizes and integrity hashes. These routing and access-control details are not end-to-end encrypted. Connection requests record who asked to add whom, when, and whether it was accepted; they never include memories. If you add a profile photo, the service stores it like your display name (not end-to-end encrypted) and shows it only to you, people you share a memory with, people you have added or who asked to add you, and people with an open invitation between you; never to someone you blocked. You can change or remove it at any time.

Your inbox records events such as invitations, acceptances and updates to shared memories, with the other person's display name. Memory titles are never included. When you allow notifications, Apple supplies a device push token, which the service stores to deliver them. The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics. Hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, technical logs and security information under their own policies. Our application does not intentionally log memory content, passwords, recovery keys or identity tokens.

Sharing

You can invite someone who has a verified Lifeline account by email, or send a one-time link. An invitation includes an encrypted preview of the title and date that only the invited person can read. A link works once, for one person, and expires after 24 hours; the key that opens it is part of the link and is never sent to our service. Anyone who has the link before it is used can accept it, so send it only to the person it is for.

Everyone who accepts a shared memory can see who else is in it and can change it. Every version is kept, so earlier versions can be restored. Private notes, tags and favourites are encrypted separately for your account. Removing, blocking or leaving a participant revokes server access and changes the keys used for future changes. Content already downloaded cannot be remotely erased. Exports you choose to share, including pictures of your story, are unencrypted and outside Lifeline's control. Saving to Photos asks for permission to add to your library; Lifeline does not read your library through that permission.

Device permissions and media

You select individual photos and videos using the system picker. Lifeline does not upload contacts or scan your photo library. Photos are resized and location metadata is removed. Video is prepared at up to 720p, and only its first five minutes are kept after your confirmation; originals in Photos are unchanged. Temporary playback and export files are protected and removed after use. App lock uses device authentication; Lifeline receives no biometric data. Place search uses Apple Maps, which receives what you type to suggest places. “Use where I am now” asks for your location once, finds the place on your iPhone, and keeps it only inside the encrypted memory. Memories you save from the Photos share sheet wait in Lifeline’s private on-device folder until you open Lifeline, which encrypts them and removes the copies.

Retention and deletion

Deleted memories have a 30-day restore window, after which the service removes their records and media. Provider backups may expire later under the provider's retention policy. When you sign in on a device that has memories kept without an account, you choose to move them into your account or delete them. Signing out removes the account's memories from the device; changes that have not finished saving stay encrypted on the device until the same account signs in again. Account deletion removes sign-in access, recovery, your profile photo and device data from the service, ends shared memberships and schedules memories you started for deletion.

Invitations expire after 30 days and links after 24 hours. Inbox history is kept for 180 days or until you delete it. Unfinished uploads expire after 24 hours.

Your choices and requests

In Settings you can manage reminders, notifications, app lock, cellular use for photos and videos, your recovery key and account deletion. Each memory offers export, sharing, history and removal. You can contact the publisher for help with access, correction or deletion of account or service information. Please do not send private memories, passwords or recovery keys.

Service providers

Firebase Authentication: Firebase privacy and security. Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2: Cloudflare privacy policy. Apple provides platform services including TestFlight, device authentication and push delivery. Providers may process service information in countries outside your own.

Changes

This policy may change as Lifeline develops. This page will show the updated date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its beta release notes.
