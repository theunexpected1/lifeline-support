Lifeline support

Keep your memories on a private timeline. Capture offline, then sync encrypted memories and share individual moments with people who accept your invitation.

For help or beta feedback, email rahul.vagadiya@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Please leave out passwords, recovery codes and private memories.

Using Lifeline

Do I need iCloud?

No. You can use Lifeline on your device without an account. Create a Lifeline account with your email and password to enable encrypted sync and sharing. Verify your email before sending or accepting invitations.

What should I do with my recovery code?

Save it somewhere safe outside Lifeline. Signing in on a new device requires both account access and your recovery code. A password reset restores sign-in access but does not recover encryption keys. Support cannot replace a lost code or decrypt your memories.

How does sharing work?

Invite a verified Lifeline account by its exact email address. The recipient reviews the invitation in Lifeline and chooses whether to accept, appear by name, add the memory to their timeline and receive updates. The owner's app must reconnect to deliver the encrypted memory key after acceptance.

Shared edits retain version history and conflicting perspectives. Private notes, tags, favourites and your chosen view stay personal. Removing or leaving a memory stops future server access, but downloaded copies cannot be remotely erased.

Why is media waiting to upload?

Media transfers use Wi-Fi by default. You can change this in Settings. The service also pauses new uploads when its shared storage budget or operation allowance approaches its limit. Text and local drafts remain saved; existing media stays available. Retry sync when connectivity or capacity is available.

Which media can I attach?

Each memory supports two photos and one video. Photos are resized with location metadata removed. Video is prepared at up to 720p. For a video longer than five minutes, Lifeline asks before retaining and uploading only its first five minutes.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open a memory's menu to export its selected view and media. Exported files are unencrypted so you can use them elsewhere; share them carefully. Settings contains Recently Deleted, personal copies, account recovery and account deletion. Deleted memories have a 30-day restore window. Deleting the app can permanently lose guest memories and unsynced changes.

Why do notifications not arrive?

Enable the categories you want in Lifeline Settings and allow notifications in iOS Settings. Shared edits also require the memory's update preference. Push delivery is best effort. Production push is available in TestFlight; local development builds do not use the production push credential.
