Using Lifeline

Do I need an account?

No. Keep your first memory and use Lifeline on one iPhone without an account. Sign in with Apple or with an email and password to keep memories across devices and share them. When you sign in on an iPhone that already has memories, you choose to move them into your account or start fresh.

What is the recovery key?

Your memories are encrypted so only you and the people you share with can open them. Your devices signed in to the same iCloud unlock them automatically. On any other device, the recovery key from Settings opens them. A password reset restores sign-in but not encryption keys, and support cannot replace a lost recovery key.

How does sharing work?

Open a memory and choose Share this memory. Invite someone who has a Lifeline account by email, choose someone you already share with, or send a one-time link that works for one person within 24 hours. When they accept, the memory appears in their story as it is, along with everyone it is shared with. Invitations and updates appear in the inbox.

Who can change a shared memory?

Everyone in a shared memory can add to it. Every version is kept, and the memory shows who edited it last. Open the history to see earlier versions and restore one. Private notes, tags and favourites stay yours.

Which photos and videos can I add?

Each memory holds two photos and one video. Photos are resized and their location data removed. Video is prepared at up to 720p; for a video longer than five minutes, Lifeline asks before keeping only the first five minutes. Originals in Photos are unchanged.

Can I share my story as a picture?

Yes. Choose Share as a picture on your story, on a person’s page or on search results. If you are looking at a filtered view, Lifeline asks whether to share what you’re seeing or your whole story. A picture holds up to 10 memories, the most recent ones. Share it or save it to Photos from the share sheet. The picture is made on your iPhone and is not encrypted once you share it.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open a memory and choose Keep a copy to export its words and media. Exported files are not encrypted, so share them carefully. Settings contains Recently deleted, Drafts and Delete account. Deleted memories can be restored for 30 days.

Why are notifications not arriving?

Turn on Invitations and shared updates in Lifeline Settings, and allow notifications for Lifeline in iOS Settings. Your inbox keeps every update even when a notification does not arrive.
